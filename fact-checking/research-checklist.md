# Fact-Checking Research Checklist

This document contains a checklist of claims and statements that require fact-checked research and verification.

- [ ] **AI Alliance for Open Innovation Foundation (AAIF) launch date and structure**
  - Verify the December 2025 launch date
  - Confirm founding member organizations
  - Validate governance structure claims

- [ ] **Open Source Initiative (OSI) AI definition process**
  - Confirm timeline for "Open Source AI" definition
  - Verify current status of discussions
  - Check user sovereignty considerations in current drafts

- [ ] **EU AI Act compliance deadlines**
  - Verify 2026 compliance deadline
  - Confirm requirements for AI providers
  - Check enforcement mechanisms

- [ ] **State attorneys general AI investigations**
  - Verify "40+ state AGs investigating AI harms" claim
  - Document specific investigations and their status
