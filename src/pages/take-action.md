## For Developers

<div class="audience-section">
  <h3 class="audience-section__title">
    <span class="audience-section__icon">&#128187;</span>
    You build the future. Help us ensure it preserves user agency.
  </h3>

  <div class="callout callout--warning" style="margin-top: 0;">
    <p class="callout__title">Why this matters now</p>
    <p>The Anthropic crisis proved that governance dependent on any single company's willingness to hold the line is governance that can be eliminated overnight. Contribute to TOI implementation so user sovereignty exists independent of any one vendor.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="grid grid--2">
    <div>
      <h4>Contribute to Framework Implementation</h4>
      <ul>
        <li>Build reference implementations of TOI, OTOI, RRT AIdvocAIte, and Sleepwalker protocols</li>
        <li>Create client libraries for framework integration</li>
        <li>Develop validation and testing tools</li>
        <li>Write documentation and examples</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
    <div>
      <h4>Build Compliant Tools</h4>
      <ul>
        <li>Integrate Solidarity Framework into your AI projects</li>
        <li>Create tools that help users generate TOI declarations</li>
        <li>Build monitoring and compliance verification systems</li>
        <li>Develop accessibility-focused interfaces</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </div>

  <h4>Join Working Groups</h4>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Specification Development</strong> - Help refine framework specifications</li>
    <li><strong>Reference Implementation</strong> - Build the canonical implementations</li>
    <li><strong>Validation & Testing</strong> - Ensure compliance can be verified</li>
    <li><strong>Integration Patterns</strong> - Document how to adopt frameworks</li>
  </ul>

  <h4>Get Started</h4>
  <ol>
    <li><a href="https://github.com/NeuroLift-Technologies/haief">Star and fork the repository</a></li>
    <li>Read the <a href="https://github.com/NeuroLift-Technologies/haief/blob/main/community/guidelines.md">contribution guidelines</a></li>
    <li>Check <a href="https://github.com/NeuroLift-Technologies/haief/issues">open issues</a> for good first contributions</li>
    <li>Join the <a href="https://github.com/NeuroLift-Technologies/haief/discussions">developer discussions</a></li>
  </ol>
</div>

## For Standards & Licensing Bodies

<div class="audience-section">
  <h3 class="audience-section__title">
    <span class="audience-section__icon">&#9878;</span>
    Your frameworks shaped software freedom. Now extend them to AI interactions.
  </h3>

  <div class="grid grid--2">
    <div>
      <h4>Open Source Initiative (OSI)</h4>
      <p>Help define "Open Source AI" with user sovereignty as a core component.</p>
      <ul>
        <li>Include interaction governance in Open Source AI Definition</li>
        <li>Recognize TOI compliance as a licensing dimension</li>
        <li>Develop user rights criteria for AI systems</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
    <div>
      <h4>Free Software Foundation (FSF)</h4>
      <p>Extend free software principles to AI interactions.</p>
      <ul>
        <li>Define "interaction freedom" as a software freedom</li>
        <li>Develop licensing approaches for AI user rights</li>
        <li>Advocate for user sovereignty in AI governance</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="grid grid--2">
    <div>
      <h4>Linux Foundation / AAIF</h4>
      <p>Complement technical standards with user representation.</p>
      <ul>
        <li>Include user sovereignty in interoperability standards</li>
        <li>Develop governance structures that include community voice</li>
        <li>Reference Solidarity Framework in AAIF specifications</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
    <div>
      <h4>W3C / IETF</h4>
      <p>Develop web standards for TOI enforcement.</p>
      <ul>
        <li>Create machine-readable TOI declaration formats</li>
        <li>Standardize AI-user interaction protocols</li>
        <li>Build accessibility requirements into AI web standards</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </div>

  <h4>Engagement Pathways</h4>
  <ul>
    <li>Invite HAIEF representatives to standards discussions</li>
    <li>Review Solidarity Framework specifications for adoption</li>
    <li>Collaborate on user rights criteria development</li>
    <li><a href="mailto:haief@neuroliftsolutions.com">Contact us for partnership discussions</a></li>
  </ul>
</div>

## For Organizations

<div class="audience-section">
  <h3 class="audience-section__title">
    <span class="audience-section__icon">&#127970;</span>
    Strategic partnership opportunity. Early adoption advantage.
  </h3>

  <div class="callout callout--warning" style="margin-top: 0;">
    <p class="callout__title">Assess your exposure</p>
    <p>If your organization relies on a single AI provider, you are now exposed to the risk that a government order — anywhere in the world — can force your provider to change its policies or be shut down entirely. The Anthropic crisis is a wake-up call to build governance infrastructure that is vendor-neutral and portable.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="grid grid--2">
    <div>
      <h4>Mission-Aligned Organizations</h4>
      <p>Partner with us to advance shared goals.</p>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>Mozilla Foundation</strong> - User agency in AI aligns with Mozilla Manifesto</li>
        <li><strong>Proton Foundation</strong> - Privacy + user rights alignment</li>
        <li><strong>Creative Commons</strong> - AI governance through CC Signals</li>
        <li><strong>EFF, EPIC, ACLU</strong> - Civil liberties in AI systems</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
    <div>
      <h4>Research Institutions</h4>

## For Users

<div class="audience-section">
  <h3 class="audience-section__title">
    <span class="audience-section__icon">&#128100;</span>
    AI should serve you, not the other way around. These are your rights.
  </h3>

  <div class="callout callout--warning" style="margin-top: 0;">
    <p class="callout__title">Your AI tools can be eliminated by political decision</p>
    <p>The Anthropic blacklisting proves that your access to AI tools — and the protections those tools provide — can be revoked by government order. Demand portable governance that travels with you, not governance locked inside a single company.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="grid grid--2">
    <div>
      <h4>Understand Your Rights</h4>
      <ul>
        <li>Learn about TOI and what user sovereignty means</li>
        <li>Understand how current AI systems fall short</li>
        <li>Know what protections you should expect</li>
        <li>Recognize when your rights are being violated</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
    <div>
      <h4>Take Action</h4>
      <ul>
        <li>Demand TOI compliance from AI providers</li>
        <li>Ask platforms about their governance frameworks</li>
        <li>Report experiences where protections failed</li>
        <li>Share your story to build the case for change</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </div>

  <h4>Join the Community</h4>
  <ul>
    <li><a href="https://github.com/NeuroLift-Technologies/haief/discussions">Participate in governance discussions</a> - No technical expertise required</li>
    <li>Share your experiences with AI systems - Your story matters</li>
    <li>Provide feedback on framework usability - Help make protections accessible</li>
    <li>Advocate for adoption by platforms you use - Your voice creates pressure</li>
  </ul>

  <p><a href="/for-humans/" class="btn btn--primary">Join the Community</a></p>
</div>
