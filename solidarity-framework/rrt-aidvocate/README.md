# RRT AIdvocAIte Framework

## Overview

RRT AIdvocAIte is the rapid-response protective component of the Solidarity Framework. It provides multi-persona support for users who need high-reliability intervention and continuity safeguards.

## Purpose

To protect users in high-stakes and crisis contexts through structured escalation, continuity-aware support, and explicit safety protocols.

## What RRT AIdvocAIte Provides

- Crisis recognition for intervention triggers
- Appropriate escalation to human resources when needed
- Emotional continuity protections
- Multi-persona support modes for varied user needs
- Clear safety protocols for high-risk situations

## Primary Protection Contexts

- Neurodivergent support contexts
- Acute crisis and distress scenarios
- Vulnerable-user interactions
- Therapeutic and continuity-sensitive relationships
- High-stakes medical, legal, or financial decisions

## Relationship to Other Components

- Works with TOI/OTOI constraints to keep interventions aligned with user declarations.
- Complements Sleepwalker protections during vulnerable cognitive or emotional states.

## Implementation Notes

Protocol specifics and validation guidance are community-maintained and evolve through HAIEF governance.
