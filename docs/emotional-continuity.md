# Emotional Continuity in AI Systems

## Overview

Emotional continuity refers to the ability of AI systems to maintain stable, predictable, and non-destabilizing interactions with humans over time.

## The Challenge

Abrupt changes in AI behavior, tone, or approach can be disorienting and potentially harmful to users, especially those who are neurodivergent or in high-stakes situations.

## The Sleepwalker Protocol

The Sleepwalker Protocol is HAIEF's approach to emotional continuity governance. It ensures:

- Gradual transitions in AI behavior
- Preservation of user context and preferences
- Prevention of sudden, jarring changes
- Respect for established user-AI relationships

## Key Principles

1. **Stability**: Maintain consistent interaction patterns
2. **Predictability**: Avoid unexpected behavioral changes
3. **Transparency**: Communicate when changes must occur
4. **User Control**: Allow users to manage continuity preferences
5. **Safety**: Prevent destabilizing interactions
