Constitutional Layer (Governance Frameworks)
TOI - Terms of Interaction

User's bill of rights. Declares preferences, boundaries, and requirements for AI interactions.

OTOI - Orchestrated Terms of Interaction

Machine-side enforcement. Ensures AI systems respect and implement user TOI declarations.

Protective Layer (Operational Protocols)
RRT AIdvocAIte

Crisis intervention and emotional continuity. Multi-persona support for vulnerable users.

Sleepwalker Protocol

Behavioral change protection. Preserves user agency during emotional or cognitive changes.
