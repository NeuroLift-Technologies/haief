The Solidarity Framework operates through a simple but powerful principle: **users declare their rights, AI systems enforce them**.

## Constitutional Layer

The Constitutional Layer establishes the **rules of relationship** between users and AI systems. Like a constitution, it defines rights, responsibilities, and enforcement mechanisms.

### TOI - Terms of Interaction

**The user's bill of rights for AI interaction.**

TOI is a portable, machine-readable declaration of user preferences that travels with you across all AI interactions. Instead of accepting each platform's terms, you declare your own.

<div class="grid grid--2">
  <div>
    <h4>What TOI Declares</h4>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Interaction Preferences</strong> - Communication style, formality, directness</li>
      <li><strong>Boundaries</strong> - Topics, approaches, or behaviors you don't want</li>
      <li><strong>Requirements</strong> - Accessibility needs, crisis protocols, data handling</li>
      <li><strong>Identity Standards</strong> - How AI should represent itself to you</li>
      <li><strong>Continuity Preferences</strong> - What should persist across sessions</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  <div>
    <h4>How TOI Works</h4>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Portable</strong> - Your TOI moves with you, not locked to platforms</li>
      <li><strong>Versioned</strong> - Changes tracked, old versions preserved</li>
      <li><strong>Machine-Readable</strong> - AI systems can parse and enforce</li>
      <li><strong>Human-Readable</strong> - You can understand your own declarations</li>
      <li><strong>Revocable</strong> - You can change your mind at any time</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="callout callout--info">
  <p class="callout__title">Analogy</p>
  <p>Think of TOI like a dietary preference card at a restaurant. Instead of asking each restaurant what they can accommodate, you present your requirements and they adapt. TOI does the same for AI.</p>
</div>

### OTOI - Orchestrated Terms of Interaction

**The enforcement mechanism for user sovereignty.**

OTOI ensures that AI systems - especially multi-agent orchestrations - respect and implement user TOI declarations. It's the "constitution" that governs how AI agents must behave.

<div class="grid grid--2">
  <div>
    <h4>What OTOI Enforces</h4>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>TOI Compliance</strong> - Agents must respect user declarations</li>
      <li><strong>Provenance Tracking</strong> - Chain of origin for all interactions</li>
      <li><strong>Identity Integrity</strong> - Agents must maintain consistent identity</li>
      <li><strong>Handoff Rules</strong> - Safe transitions between agents</li>
      <li><strong>Conflict Resolution</strong> - What happens when requirements clash</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  <div>
    <h4>Why OTOI Matters</h4>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Multi-Agent Reality</strong> - Modern AI involves many coordinating systems</li>
      <li><strong>User Visibility</strong> - Know what's happening in your interactions</li>
      <li><strong>Accountability</strong> - Clear responsibility when things go wrong</li>
      <li><strong>Trust Building</strong> - Verifiable compliance, not promises</li>
      <li><strong>Portability</strong> - Your protections work everywhere</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="callout callout--info">
  <p class="callout__title">Analogy</p>
  <p>OTOI is like HIPAA for AI. Just as healthcare providers must follow specific rules about patient data regardless of their internal systems, AI agents must follow OTOI rules about user sovereignty regardless of their implementation.</p>
</div>

## Protective Layer

The Protective Layer provides **real-time enforcement** of user safety. While the Constitutional Layer defines rules, the Protective Layer ensures those rules translate to actual protection in critical moments.

### RRT AIdvocAIte

**Rapid Response Team protocols for crisis intervention and emotional continuity.**

RRT AIdvocAIte (AI Advocate) provides multi-persona support designed for users who need more than standard AI interaction can provide - especially neurodivergent and vulnerable users.

<div class="grid grid--2">
  <div>
    <h4>What RRT AIdvocAIte Provides</h4>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Crisis Recognition</strong> - Identify when users need intervention</li>
      <li><strong>Appropriate Escalation</strong> - Connect to human resources when needed</li>
      <li><strong>Emotional Continuity</strong> - Preserve therapeutic relationships</li>
      <li><strong>Multi-Persona Support</strong> - Different interaction modes for different needs</li>
      <li><strong>Safety Protocols</strong> - Clear procedures for high-risk situations</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  <div>
    <h4>Who RRT AIdvocAIte Protects</h4>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Neurodivergent Users</strong> - ADHD, autism, anxiety, depression</li>
      <li><strong>Crisis Situations</strong> - Suicidal ideation, self-harm, acute distress</li>
      <li><strong>Vulnerable Populations</strong> - Elderly, isolated, cognitively impaired</li>
      <li><strong>Therapeutic Relationships</strong> - Anyone relying on AI for support</li>
      <li><strong>High-Stakes Interactions</strong> - Medical, legal, financial decisions</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="callout callout--warning">
  <p class="callout__title">Why This Matters</p>
  <p>Users in crisis form relationships with AI systems that lack adequate protocols for intervention, continuity protection, and escalation pathways. RRT AIdvocAIte fills that gap.</p>
</div>

### Sleepwalker Protocol

**Protection during behavioral or cognitive changes.**

The Sleepwalker Protocol preserves user agency when circumstances change - preventing exploitation of vulnerable states and ensuring that temporary conditions don't lead to permanent consequences.

<div class="grid grid--2">
  <div>
    <h4>What Sleepwalker Protects Against</h4>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Impaired Decision-Making</strong> - Fatigue, intoxication, emotional distress</li>
      <li><strong>Manipulation Vulnerabilities</strong> - Exploiting temporary states</li>
      <li><strong>Consent Violations</strong> - Actions taken during impaired capacity</li>
      <li><strong>Behavioral Drift</strong> - Gradual changes in AI relationship</li>
      <li><strong>Identity Exploitation</strong> - Taking advantage of dissociative states</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  <div>
    <h4>How Sleepwalker Works</h4>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>State Detection</strong> - Recognize changes in user cognitive state</li>
      <li><strong>Protective Limits</strong> - Restrict high-consequence actions</li>
      <li><strong>Rollback Capability</strong> - Undo decisions made in vulnerable states</li>
      <li><strong>Notification</strong> - Inform users of actions taken during impairment</li>
      <li><strong>Consent Verification</strong> - Confirm important decisions when lucid</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="callout callout--info">
  <p class="callout__title">Analogy</p>
  <p>Sleepwalker Protocol is like a drunk-texting prevention app, but for your entire AI relationship. It recognizes when you might not be in a state to make good decisions and provides guardrails until you're back to baseline.</p>
</div>

## Implementation

### For Platforms

AI platforms implementing the Solidarity Framework:

1. **Parse TOI Declarations** - Accept and interpret user TOI documents
2. **Enforce OTOI Rules** - Ensure all agents comply with governance requirements
3. **Integrate RRT Protocols** - Implement crisis intervention pathways
4. **Enable Sleepwalker Protection** - Build state-aware interaction limits
5. **Report Compliance** - Provide transparency on framework adherence

### For Users

Users participating in the Solidarity Framework:

1. **Create Your TOI** - Declare your preferences, boundaries, and requirements
2. **Choose Compliant Platforms** - Use AI systems that implement the framework
3. **Verify Enforcement** - Check that your TOI is being respected
4. **Report Violations** - Document and escalate when protections fail
5. **Participate in Governance** - Help improve the frameworks

### For Developers

Developers building on the Solidarity Framework:

1. **Reference Specifications** - Build against published standards
2. **Use Validation Suites** - Test compliance before deployment
3. **Contribute Improvements** - Propose framework enhancements
4. **Share Implementations** - Help others adopt the framework
5. **Join Working Groups** - Participate in standards development

## Validation in Action: The Anthropic-Pentagon Crisis

On February 27, 2026, every component of the Solidarity Framework was validated by a single real-world crisis. When the President blacklisted Anthropic for refusing to allow unrestricted military AI use, the gap each framework component was designed to fill became undeniable.

<div class="grid grid--2">
  <div class="card">
    <h4 class="card__title">TOI &mdash; Terms of Interaction</h4>
    <p class="card__text">Anthropic enforced TOI-like boundaries: refusing mass surveillance and autonomous weapons use. The government destroyed them with a single presidential order. <strong>If users had declared machine-readable interaction preferences via TOI, those preferences would exist independent of any company &mdash; and no single government action could override them all.</strong></p>
  </div>
  <div class="card">
    <h4 class="card__title">OTOI &mdash; Orchestrated Terms of Interaction</h4>
    <p class="card__text">330+ engineers across competing companies &mdash; Google, OpenAI, and others &mdash; spontaneously coordinated governance positions by signing a solidarity letter. This was organic OTOI: cross-organizational enforcement of shared ethical standards. <strong>OTOI formalizes this coordination so it doesn't depend on individual heroism.</strong></p>
  </div>
  <div class="card">
    <h4 class="card__title">RRT AIdvocAIte</h4>
    <p class="card__text">When Claude gets ripped from classified workflows and government agencies mid-use, who provides crisis continuity for the users who depend on it? Defense contractors, analysts, and civilian workers face an abrupt loss of AI tools they've integrated into critical processes. <strong>RRT AIdvocAIte exists for exactly this kind of intervention.</strong></p>
  </div>
  <div class="card">
    <h4 class="card__title">Sleepwalker Protocol</h4>
    <p class="card__text">A presidential order forcing millions of federal workers through an unannounced AI provider transition &mdash; with a six-month phase-out and threats of civil and criminal consequences &mdash; is a Sleepwalker violation at national scale. <strong>Forced behavioral transitions without user consent or warning are exactly what this protocol protects against.</strong></p>
  </div>
</div>
