## Community Guidelines

Our community operates on principles of respect, accessibility, and agency.

### Respect

- **Listen to understand**, not just to respond
- **Assume good faith** - most disagreements aren't personal
- **Respect lived experience** - credentials aren't required for valid input
- **Honor boundaries** - if someone says "not for me," believe them

### Accessibility

- **Plain language welcome** - not everyone speaks tech
- **Multiple formats accepted** - text, audio, whatever works
- **Pacing flexibility** - not everyone can respond quickly
- **Accommodations available** - ask and we'll try to help

### Agency

- **Participation is voluntary** - no obligation beyond what you choose
- **Opinions are your own** - you represent yourself, not a group
- **Exit is always available** - you can step back anytime
- **Attribution is respected** - your contributions credited as you prefer

### What We Don't Accept

- Personal attacks or harassment
- Discrimination based on any identity
- Dismissing input because of who it comes from
- Corporate astroturfing or undisclosed conflicts

## Governance Participation

HAIEF governance is community-driven. Here's how decisions get made:

### Proposal Process

1. **Draft** - Anyone can write a proposal using our template
2. **Discussion** - Community reviews and provides feedback
3. **Revision** - Proposer incorporates feedback
4. **Decision** - Working group or community reaches consensus
5. **Implementation** - Approved proposals get built

### Decision Making

- **Consensus-seeking** - We try to find solutions everyone can support
- **Transparent** - All discussions and decisions are public
- **Accountable** - Decision rationale is documented
- **Revisable** - Decisions can be reconsidered with new information

### Your Voice Matters

In HAIEF, the strength of an argument matters more than the credentials of the speaker. We specifically value:

- **Lived experience** with AI systems
- **Perspectives** from affected communities
- **Practical insight** about what users actually need
- **Fresh thinking** unburdened by industry assumptions
